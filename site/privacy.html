Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, the name you enter, and a password (stored securely by our database provider; we never see it).
  • Training data: lesson and placement results, answers to scenarios, XP, streaks and levels.
  • On your device only: your goal, daily target and reminder time. These are not sent to us.

We do not use advertising, ad tracking, or third-party analytics, and we do not sell your data.

How we use it

Only to run the app: saving your progress, calculating streaks and levels, and letting you sign in on another device.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Supabase, our database and authentication provider, which processes it on our behalf.

Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. You can turn them off in the app (Profile) or in iPhone Settings.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time in the app: Profile > Delete account. Deletion is immediate.
