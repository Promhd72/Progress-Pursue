Rapid Math

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Rapid Math is a mental maths training app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We collect as little as we can.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, the name you enter, and a password. Passwords are stored in hashed form by our database provider; we can't read them.
  • Training data: lesson and placement results, scenario answers, XP, streaks and levels.
  • Subscription status: whether Rapid Math Pro is active and when it renews or ends. Payments are handled by Apple. We never see your card or payment details.
  • Messages you send us: feedback and feature requests, with your email, app version and device type so we can reply and fix problems.

Stored on your device only

Your goal, daily target, reminder time, confidence check-ins, warm-up scores and which tips you've read stay on your phone and are not sent to us.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No third-party analytics.
  • We don't sell or rent your data.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.

How we use your data

Only to run the app: saving your progress, working out streaks and levels, unlocking Pro, letting you sign in on another device, and replying to your messages.

Who helps us run the app

  • Supabase: stores your account and training data and handles sign-in.
  • Apple: processes subscription payments through the App Store.
  • RevenueCat: confirms your subscription status with Apple. It receives an anonymous account ID and your purchase history for this app, not your name or email.

These providers process data on our behalf and only for these purposes.

Notifications

Daily reminders are optional and scheduled on your iPhone. Turn them off any time in Profile or in iPhone Settings.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while you have an account. You can permanently delete your account and everything linked to it, including messages you sent us, in the app: Profile > Delete account. Deletion is immediate and can't be undone. Deleting your account doesn't cancel an App Store subscription: cancel it in iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions.

Children

Rapid Math is made for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.

Contact

Email support@example.com, or message us in the app: Profile > Send feedback.
